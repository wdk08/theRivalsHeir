The Rival's Heir

Written by William Kapadia

Summary

The Rival's Heir follows Sophia Moretti and Luke Callahan, two young lovers born into rival organized crime families. Despite a bitter feud between their fathers, their childhood friendship grows into a forbidden romance. But when their relationship threatens everything their families stand for, Sophia and Luke must choose between the lives they've always known and a future together, even if escaping means risking everything.
